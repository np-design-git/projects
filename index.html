Overview

Desa Alam Website

Proposal for the design and Webflow development of Desa Alam's investor-facing website: a multi-page digital presence communicating the project vision, investment opportunity, and team behind a luxury resort development on Lombok, Indonesia.

Proposal

Design Objectives

01
Silent luxury
Visual language in the spirit of Aman Resorts, editorial, calm, confident
02
Elevated design
A website that feels refined from day one, reflecting the ambition of the project.
03
Selling with confidence
Let the project speak for itself. The investment opportunity is presented with elegance, not pressure.

Proposal

Scope Limitations

Language
English only: single language, no localization
Content
Final copy, photography, and renders provided by the client. I advise on structure and content needs, and support with AI-generated imagery and copy drafts as needed.
SEO
Webflow outputs clean, fast, SEO-friendly code. Keyword strategy, content SEO, and ongoing optimisation are not included.
Analytics
Google Analytics can be connected via a simple embed at no extra cost. Event tracking, conversion funnels, and monitoring are not part of the scope.
Domain
The client is responsible for purchasing their own domain.
Investor Brochure
Design and layout of the downloadable PDF is not included in this scope, as the complexity and extent of the document is not yet defined. Depending on requirements, this can be scoped as a separate add-on.
