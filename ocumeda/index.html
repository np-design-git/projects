Overview

OCUMEDA Website

Proposal for the design and Framer implementation of Ocumeda's website redesign.

Proposal

Design Objectives

01
The New CI
Applying the new CI makes the website fresh, warm and human, without feeling clinical.
02
Warm Med-tech
Approachable and trustworthy: medical expertise with a human face. "Med-tech that radiates warmth".
03
Show the Product
Showcase the product and tell a clear story with mockups and photography instead of abstract animations.
Proposal

Scope of Work

Style Guide & UI Library
The new CI is applied across the whole website, through a style guide and UI library.
Content & Structure
Refined content and structure for the four main pages, based on the structure provided by Ocumeda.
Components
The new structure gets the building blocks it needs. The UI library is extended with new components for the redesigned pages.
Graphics
New graphics and product mockups based on the new brand guidelines and the new photography style, as the main pages need them.
Main Pages
Structural redesign of Home, Doctors, Opticians and Customers, according to the new content and storyline.
QA
Pre-launch QA
Proposal

Scope Limitations

Animations
No new complex animations
Copy
Copy content is supplied by Ocumeda. Writing the copy is out of scope.
Translations
Translations are not included.
Secondary Pages
Team, Career and News get no structural or content changes. The new CI still reaches them through the components and style guide.
